- [Prior work](prior-work.md) — the wave-digital Scattering Iterative Method (Bernardini et al. 2018) is essentially this algorithm; position novelty claims against it; Scellier 2024 coordinate descent = nonlinear Gauss-Seidel on the circuit's energy
- [Measurement machine](measurement-machine.md) — two machines: 8-core Lunar Lake laptop (15.5 GB, multithreaded BLAS slows CHOLMOD; on battery it throttles ~3x after ~10 s, so time only on AC) and 6-core i5 PC (31.8 GB, threads help a little at 1024); always name the machine, never "this machine"; never compare across them
- [1D1R findings](diode-1d1r-findings.md) — leakless 1D1R: alpha=1 fails, iterations grow with size under R_min matching (all-reverse rows), oracle open-circuit matching restores ~30 flat; tanh not exercised >= 64x64; SPICE (plain Newton, 6-12 its) beats it from ~128x128
- [1S1R findings](selector-1s1r-findings.md) — threshold selector + memristor: the knee doesn't hurt Newton; at scale Spingarn (sector-matched, ~110/~220 its flat) beats SPICE from 512 (1.9-2.6x), SPICE hits the KLU limit at 768
- [Condat-Vu findings](condat-vu-findings.md) — paper's Algorithm 1 on the tanh crossbar: iterations ~17||M||, ||M|| ~ 0.90(n+1/2) closed form, rate = 1/(1+tau R_min); ~230x slower than Spingarn at 256 (default), fastest solver up to 64 on a high-R crossbar; tree/rho/stopping-rule lessons; 1D1R prediction
- [Scellier CD findings](scellier-findings.md) — 1D1R sweep 2026-10-13 (laptop): node-by-node coordinate descent ~10^5 sweeps at 64 (unusable), word/bit line blocks tie Spingarn (38 vs 39 s at 256), SPICE fastest (13 s), Condat-Vu 76 s
